# Skills
> - Timers aren't a thing, only **stopwatches** are
> - Beakers can't measure things, only **measuring cylinders** can
> - Micrometer **screwgauge**

## General 
### "Reduce uncertainty"
- Repeat experiment and calculate a mean
    - ...to reduce the impact of random error
- Repeat experiment to detect anomalies
    - ...to be discarded

## Oscilloscope
### "Reduce uncertainty"
- Use a smaller (time-base / voltage-gain)
    - ...so the waveform will stretch (horizontally / vertically)
- ...then, the resolution of the (time/ voltage) axis has increased
- ...and measuring more divisions reduces percentage uncertainty
> Provide a numerical factor for any stretches

## Stuff with graduations
### "Reduce uncertainty"
- View at right angles at eye level
    - to reduce the impact of parallax error
- (liquids) Read from the bottom of the meniscus

## Stuff with a zero button
- Ensure it is zeroed before use
- (balance) Consider if you want to zero it with things on it or not

## Measuring the diameter of long things
- Repeat measurements/readings at different **points** along the rod in different **directions**, and calculate a mean
- ...and discard anomalies as before

## Radiation
### Background radiation
- Remove all radioactive sources from the room
- Zero the counter and stopwatch
- Measure it on the same day of the experiment at the same location
- Use a longer time (at least 100s)

## "Make sure X is parallel to Y"
- Use set square(s) to align with Y
- Allow for continuous variation of X, if required
